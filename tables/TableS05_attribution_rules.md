# Supplementary Table S5. Storms behind regional landslide events under alternative attribution rules.

Shares of regional landslide events (at least 20 landslides) under an AR or a TC, with the number of events in brackets. The main rule attributes an event to a TC when a TC with winds of at least 34 kt lay within 500 km between two days before the event and its last day, otherwise to an AR when an AR covered the centre cell between the day before the event and its last day. The peak-day rule uses the peak day of the event inventories, for which it is known.

| Attribution rule | Cold season, poleward of 25°: AR (%) | Warm season, poleward of 25°: AR (%) | Tropics: AR (%) | Tropics: TC (%) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Main: TC within 500 km, AR at the centre cell | 95 (115) | 52 (65) | 18 (51) | 39 |
| TC within 300 km | 95 (115) | 54 (65) | 18 (51) | 37 |
| TC within 800 km | 95 (115) | 52 (65) | 18 (51) | 41 |
| AR anywhere in the 3 × 3 cells | 95 (115) | 63 (65) | 18 (51) | 39 |
| AR on the peak day (event inventories) | 95 (115) | 52 (65) | 18 (51) | 39 |
| No TC rule | 96 (115) | 57 (65) | 20 (51) | 0 |
