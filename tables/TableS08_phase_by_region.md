# Supplementary Table S8. Phase of cold-season AR extreme precipitation over mountains by region, 1941–2024.

ERA5 0.25° cells at or above 300 m in each box (land only); AR extreme cell-days have 2-day precipitation at or above the local 99th percentile (1979–2022) and an AR on the same or the previous day, and are snow-dominated when less than half of the 2-day precipitation fell as rain. Trends are least-squares slopes of the annual values with their *P* values; snow-dominated land is the share of the assessed mountain area (cells with at least ten AR extreme cell-days in the period) on which at least half of these cell-days were snow-dominated. The cold season is October–March in the Northern Hemisphere and April–September in the Southern Hemisphere.
