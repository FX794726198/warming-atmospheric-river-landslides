# Supplementary Table S3. Observed and chance AR shares of landslide events.

The chance share applies the attribution rule of each set of events to the events moved to the same calendar dates in every other year, with their cells (or centre) and duration; the range is the 2.5–97.5 percentile of 1,000 draws of one year per event. Inventory events are AR events when at least half of their cell-days are under an AR; regional events when an AR covered the centre between the day before the event and its last day (events with a TC within 500 km are counted as TC events, and among the regional events only). Inventory events are the 14,319 pooled events that ended by 2022, the window of the ERA5 rain data; the eight United States events of 2023–2024 are left out. For the regional events, the chance share is that of AR exposure at the centre under the AR rule in the other years; events with a TC keep their TC label in the observed share, and the TC rule is not re-applied in the other years.
