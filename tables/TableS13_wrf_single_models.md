# Supplementary Table S13. Western Norway 2011 with the change of single CMIP6 models.

Runs with the December change for 2071–2100 relative to 1985–2014 under SSP5-8.5 of one CMIP6 model (one member), ordered by the simulated warming, and the run with the multi-model mean change. Warming is the simulated change in 2-m temperature over land in the 3 km domain, averaged over the event; the area is the land area with at least 100 mm of rain in 48 h (203 km² in the control run) and the area ratio its ratio to the control run; precipitation, rain and snowfall ratios are those of the land-mean event totals; the rain share is the share of the precipitation above 1,000 m that fell as rain (18% in the control run). All statistics leave out ten cells along the domain edges.
