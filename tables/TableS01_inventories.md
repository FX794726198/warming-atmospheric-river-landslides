# Supplementary Table S1. Landslide inventories.

Landslides are those within the given period after removal of earthquake-triggered landslides (United States) and of landslides without a rain trigger (global inventories). Events join landslides of the same inventory within one day and in neighbouring 1° cells. Landslides under an AR had an AR over their cell on the same or the previous day; AR frequency is the frequency of the same two-day window in the same cells and calendar months. Analyses that use ERA5 rain or temperature use the events that ended by 2022 (3,141 of the 3,149 United States events).
