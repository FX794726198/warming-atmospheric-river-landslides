Values of the control run with, in brackets, the change in the warmer run (for areas, the ratio of the warmer to the control run), over land in the 3 km domains, leaving out ten cells along the edges, area-weighted. Warming is the change in the simulated 2-m temperature over land in the 3 km domain, averaged over the event, and peak IVT the maximum over the 9 km domain. The warmer run is the run with the full change. Rain is liquid precipitation (total precipitation minus snow, graupel and hail), and areas are land areas with at least the given 48-h rain.
