# Supplementary Table S12. WRF runs at four levels of warming.

Control runs and runs with 0.25, 0.5, 0.75 and 1 times the CMIP6 multi-model mean change for 2071–2100 under SSP5-8.5. Warming is the simulated change in 2-m temperature over land in the 3 km domain, averaged over the event. Areas are land areas with at least the given 48-h rain, and the CC reference is the area with at least 100 mm when the largest 48-h rain of the control run is scaled at every cell by 7% per degree of the warming of the run, with its pattern and phase unchanged. The rain share is the share of the precipitation above 1,000 m that fell as rain, and the rain–snow line is the elevation at which half of the precipitation in the storm core (land with at least 100 mm of precipitation in the control run) fell as snow, interpolated between 100 m elevation bands (– where the snow share stayed below one half). Population (GHS-POP 2020) and roads (GRIP4) are those inside the land area with at least 100 mm of rain in 48 h. All statistics leave out ten cells along the domain edges. For the 0.25 and 0.75 steps of Liguria and Piedmont and of Rio Grande do Sul, only the warming, the areas with at least 100 mm and the rain share were computed (n.a., not available).
