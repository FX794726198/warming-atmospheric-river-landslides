# Supplementary Table S11. Precipitation phase and the landslide sites in the WRF storms.

Snow-dominated land is land with at least 25 mm of precipitation in the control run on which at least half of the event precipitation fell as snow, graupel or hail; the share turned to rain is the part of it on which most precipitation fell as rain in the warmer run (full SSP5-8.5 change). The rain–snow line is the elevation at which half of the precipitation in the storm core (land with at least 100 mm of precipitation in the control run) fell as snow, interpolated between 100 m elevation bands (– where the snow share stayed below one half). The last columns give the share of the landslides mapped for the storm that lay inside the land with at least 100 mm of rain in 48 h, and the median largest 48-h rain at the landslide sites, in the control and warmer runs. All statistics leave out ten cells along the domain edges.
