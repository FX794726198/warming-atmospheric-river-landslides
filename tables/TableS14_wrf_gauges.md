# Supplementary Table S14. Control runs against rain gauges.

Event precipitation at gauges of the Global Historical Climatology Network–Daily with complete daily records inside the 3 km domain (leaving out ten cells along the edges), against the precipitation of the control run at the nearest land grid point within 5 km over the same days; in British Columbia, the four 24-h periods start at 12 UTC because the run ends at 12 UTC on 16 November. Mean bias is the mean simulated total over the mean observed total minus one; the share within a factor of two counts gauges with at least 1 mm observed. The gauges lie in Norway (251), Sweden (135), Denmark and the United Kingdom (3) for western Norway, in the United States (590) and Canada (168) for British Columbia, and in Germany (58), France (24), Italy (17), Switzerland (9), Slovenia (7) and Austria (4) for Liguria and Piedmont, and in the United States (375) for Oregon. For Rio Grande do Sul, where no gauge of the network had complete records, the row compares the 0.05° cells of CHIRPS instead, and the bias is that of the area mean.
